Financial Operations

Operational Accounting

System-Independent Finance Integration for Tour Operators

A system-independent financial operations application that synchronizes with any travel system and can post approved financial data to any advanced accounting system. It centralizes supplier invoices, agency payments, cash advances and settlements, expense sheets, exchange rates, and approvals — reducing duplicate entry and keeping finance aligned with operations.

Any Travel System Synchronization

Synchronize suppliers, agencies, reservation files, and service details from any travel system into one accounting workflow.

Supplier Invoice Management

Create, import, review, and track supplier invoices by voucher, reservation file, or PNR.

Payments & Expense Control

Manage agency payments, expense sheets, cash advances, settlements, banks, and exchange rates in one place.

Approval & Accounting-System Posting

Control approvals and post approved financial transactions to any advanced accounting system.
